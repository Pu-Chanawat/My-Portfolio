RECORD #001 · SECURITY

Cybersecurity CTF Competition 2025

14 June 2025 · Bangkok · Team of 2 · Ranked Top 10

Overview

A one-day capture-the-flag event with roughly thirty teams competing across web exploitation, forensics, cryptography, and reverse engineering categories. My teammate and I worked through the web and forensics tracks, submitting flags until the closing buzzer.

My role

  • Reconnaissance and attack-surface mapping for the web challenges
  • Manual exploitation of a broken access-control flaw
  • File-carving and metadata analysis for two forensics challenges
  • Time-boxing the strategy so we never lost a whole hour on one flag

What I learned

Speed mattered less than process. The flags we missed were the ones where we skipped writing down what we had already tried. Since then I keep a scratch log for every challenge — it has paid off in every event after.

I also came away with a much better feel for how quickly a small misconfiguration in a web app becomes a full compromise.

Timeline

  • 10:00 — Competition opens; 24 challenges released
  • 13:30 — First web flag captured
  • 16:00 — Forensics category cleared
  • 17:00 — Buzzer; results announced
